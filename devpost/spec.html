How a nudge decides where to go

WhatsApp only lets an app write freely to someone who messaged it in the last 24 hours, and a sandbox membership lasts 3 days. So the app checks before every nudge.

The app asks
Did this person message the sandbox in the last 24 hours, and join in the last 3 days?
Yes
Send by WhatsApp. Sarah's line plus today's task arrives on your phone.
No
Send the same message by email. Home shows a short notice with the code word to reconnect.

What was simplified

Free WhatsApp sandbox instead of a real WhatsApp sender

Free and certain to work for the demo. People join with a code word and rejoin every 3 days. A real sender needs a Meta business account and verification that can take several weeks.

Email stands in when WhatsApp is blocked

The sandbox can't message someone who has been quiet for 24 hours, which is exactly the missed-day case. Email keeps the no-guilt sequence reaching people, in the same words.

No final voice note

It can't be delivered in the sandbox and would need a text-to-speech service. It moves to possible later enhancements.

No "confirm your email" step at sign-up

Supabase's built-in confirmation emails are tightly limited on the free plan.

Each task's "why" is written along with the task

Opening the ? is instant and costs nothing, and Georgina explains her reasoning while she still has it.

Missed tasks roll forward when you open the app or your nudge is due

Same result as doing it at midnight, with no extra scheduled job.

Nothing at the heart of the idea is faked. Tasks are written by the AI for your goal, the nudge is a real WhatsApp message on a real phone, and progress comes from tasks you marked done.

Where everything lives

The important boundary is lib/. Screens never talk to Claude, Twilio or Resend directly. They ask lib/, and lib/ does the talking.

scaffold/
├── app/                  # the screens, one folder per menu item
│   ├── (auth)/           # sign-up, log-in
│   ├── (app)/            # Home, Progress, Plan, Team, Profile, onboarding
│   └── api/              # doors other services knock on
│       ├── chat/             # a teammate's reply
│       ├── cron/tick/        # the 15-minute bell
│       └── whatsapp/inbound/ # your WhatsApp replies
├── components/           # reusable parts of screens
│   ├── ui/  shell/  chat/  task/
│   ├── house/            # the house drawing
│   └── celebrate/        # confetti, stamp, ribbon
├── lib/                  # the rules and the outside world
│   ├── ai/               # the four teammates, the plan builder
│   ├── tasks/            # mark done, roll forward, weeks and streaks
│   ├── nudges/           # who is due, which channel, send
│   └── supabase/         # reading and writing the database
├── supabase/migrations/  # the table definitions
├── SCAFFOLD_DESIGN_HANDOFF.md  # your design handoff
└── devpost/              # these planning docs

What gets saved

Each is one table in Supabase, which works like a spreadsheet. You can only ever see your own rows.

About you

  • profiles: name, WhatsApp, nudge time, weekly target, houses built
  • week_records: each week's target and count
  • dreams, path_steps

Kept when you abandon a project.

About one house

  • projects: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, if–then plans
  • milestones: the five stages
  • tasks, proofs (notes and photos)
  • conversations, messages, plan_proposals

Deleted together when you abandon the project.

Worked out, never stored

  • Today's task
  • Bricks laid
  • Current stage
  • Streak

Counted fresh each time, so they can't drift out of step.

Still to check during the build

  • Email to other people. Resend may need a domain you own (about $10 a year) before it will send to addresses other than yours. If so, friends would get WhatsApp-only nudges until one is connected.
  • The 15-minute bell on Supabase's free plan, and whether it stops the project pausing after a quiet week.
  • Vercel's time limit against a slow plan from Opus. If it's too short, the plan is requested in two smaller parts.
  • Screens not designed yet: sign-up and log-in, Home after today's task is done, Profile before a goal, and the wording of WhatsApp and email messages. Each is decided at its build step.
