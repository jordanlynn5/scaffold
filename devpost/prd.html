Scaffold

You're the builder, and you have a team behind you. Scaffold's AI build team turns your dream into one small task a day, nudges you on WhatsApp, and shows your house going up, brick by brick.

Success feels like

Capable of setting, sticking to, and finishing a goal. Never overwhelmed, never guilted.

When you miss days

The no-guilt sequence. "Activity" means completing a task. Opening the app or chatting doesn't count.

  • Any missed taskIt rolls forward: tomorrow shows the same task and the plan shifts by one day. No red, no blame.
  • Day 1 missedThe normal WhatsApp nudge, gentle about yesterday.
  • Day 2 missedSmaller and warmer: "just 5 minutes today?"
  • Day 3 missedSarah invites a chat with the team to adjust the plan.
  • After day 3Daily nudges stop.
  • Start of next week, still nothing doneA sincere email reminding you why you started.
  • ThenSilence.
  • Any task completed, at any pointEverything resets. Daily nudges resume the next day.

What if?

SituationWhat happens
You haven't set a goal yetHome shows an empty lot: "Every dream house starts with a plan." Every menu shows a friendly empty state pointing to Alice.
You don't know what to buildAlice helps you find a goal. A big dream is broken into a path of smaller goals, each its own house.
You leave onboarding halfwayEverything is saved. Home says "Welcome back" and offers "Continue where you left off."
You hit some days but not your targetNo message at all.
You lower your target below what you've doneNo confetti from the setting change. The celebration comes with the next task you mark done that week.
One task finishes a stage and hits your targetThe milestone celebration first, then the weekly one after you close it. Never two at once.
A teammate suggests changing the planYou see the old and new task side by side. Nothing changes until you confirm. You can decline.
You change your goalA warning lists what is deleted forever (house, plan, WOOP answers, learning log, team chats) and what you keep (account, settings, houses built). You must press "Yes, I want to abandon this project."
You finish the houseThe scaffolding comes down, you cut the ribbon yourself, and your houses-built count goes up for good.
You say you did a task you didn'tThe app trusts you. Proof is optional and never checked.

What gets built, in order

Screens first, then WhatsApp. Your reason: "the WhatsApp nudge isn't valuable without the rest."

Core first · submittable after step 8
  1. Foundations: the look, the menu, shared pieces
  2. Sign up and log in
  3. Home before a goal, and the other empty states
  4. Onboarding + WOOP, saved as you go
  5. Roadmap + first 2 weeks, review, commit
  6. Home: today's task, mark done, note or photo
  7. Progress: the house, roadmap, weekly target
  8. Weekly target celebration
Then layered on
  1. Plan view, learning log, missed tasks rolling forward
  2. Talk to Team with confirmed plan changes
  3. Profile and the abandon flow
  4. Milestone and house-complete celebrations
  5. Next 2-week plan generation my placement
  6. Goal-finder chat, dreams, and paths
  7. Daily WhatsApp nudge
  8. Two-way WhatsApp: replying with proof
  9. Missed-day nudges, email backup, sincere email
  10. Mastery tasks my placement
  11. "Why this task" explanations my placement

Left out on purpose

  • Automatically adjusting tasks to how you feel that day
  • Dark mode
  • Tracking other apps' usage (replaced by a Screen Time suggestion)
  • Verifying proof
  • Guilt or punishment of any kind

Possible later

  • Links to real, checkable sources behind each "why this task"
  • Day 1 vs. day 20 proof comparison
  • Email as a cheaper nudge at large scale
  • Several goals at once

Decisions you made today

Screens before WhatsApp

"The WhatsApp nudge isn't valuable without the rest."

No separate levels or rewards

"The bricks and stages are the levels and rewards."

Refreshers are "mastery" tasks

"A refresher would appear as any other task, but we can call it mastery."

Georgina decides when to include one.

Tasks are 20 minutes or less

"Tasks should be kept to 20 minutes and under."

Up from 5 to 15. Example: 6 hands, 3 minutes each.

"Why this task" on request

"Why 10 and why 3. What is it based on."

A ? next to the task. Georgina explains only when you ask.
