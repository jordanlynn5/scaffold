Dream Friend (working title)

A friend who keeps you on track with a goal you care about. She gives you one clear task each day and nudges you on WhatsApp to do it.

What changed from draft 1: the problem moved from "I don't know where to start" to "I know what to do, but I stop doing it." Planning is now just setup. The heart is a clear daily task and a nudge that reaches you.

The kernel

Clear, assigned tasks plus a nudge that reaches you. One small, finishable task a day, a WhatsApp reminder that includes your own why, and visible progress, Duolingo-style.

"I have a goal and I might even have a great plan, but if I'm not working on it I'll never achieve it."
"I either did the task or I didn't."

The core loop

Who it's for

Someone who wants to get better, but drifts

They enjoy something and want to improve, but struggle to be consistent without clear, assigned tasks. Books and videos help until they run out or life gets busy.

First real user: you, the illustrator

"I really enjoy art and creating it, but I struggle to be consistent when I don't have clear, assigned tasks." Built so anyone with a goal could use it.

The boundary

Now In the proof of concept
  • One-time setup: goal → why → available time
  • A clear, small daily task the friend assigns toward the goal
  • Marking the task done: did it or didn't
  • A visible progress indicator (streak or path)
  • A WhatsApp nudge to your own phone with today's task and your why, using the Twilio WhatsApp Sandbox (a free test setup)
Later Worth doing, not now
  • Replying "done" directly in WhatsApp
  • Refreshers on things you learned earlier
  • A log of what you learned from each task
  • Real, cited sources behind tasks, so she never gives bad advice
  • Adjusting tasks to how you're feeling that day
  • Fuller gamification: levels, rewards
  • A follow-up conversation that adjusts the next task
  • Planning brand-new goals from scratch (e.g. the AI group for women in Gijón)
Cut Out of the PoC, and why
  • Two-way WhatsApp receiving replies needs the app to be reachable from the internet. One-way nudges prove the idea.
  • Real-sources research it matters, but the focus moved to follow-through and WhatsApp took that time. First in line for later.
  • Refreshers & learning log valuable, but not needed to prove "a clear task plus a nudge keeps me going."
  • Production WhatsApp business approval to message anyone isn't needed; the sandbox reaches your own phone.
